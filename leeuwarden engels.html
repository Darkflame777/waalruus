De Walrus Leeuwarden

Grand-Café De Walrus is located at a beautiful spot on the Gouverneursplein in the heart of Leeuwarden. Over the years, the buildings have served various functions. The Leeuwarder Bankvereeniging, the Bearda furniture factory, and the ‘t Centrum library were all predecessors of De Walrus, which opened its doors here in 2009.
Grand-Café De Walrus is the perfect place for a delicious cup of coffee with pastries, to have lunch during a day out in Leeuwarden, or for drinks with friends or colleagues. Looking for a great restaurant in Leeuwarden? De Walrus Leeuwarden is the right place for you. The atmospheric restaurant on the first floor offers a wonderful view over the center of Leeuwarden.
Are you looking for a venue to dine with a large group as the grand finale of a company outing, family day, or special occasion in Friesland? De Walrus Leeuwarden offers various packages, allowing you to enjoy a walking dinner or a set menu.
Spending a weekend in Leeuwarden, looking to wrap up a day of shopping, or simply catch up with friends or family? Enjoy a fully catered high tea with various types of tea or coffee, savory bites, and sweet treats. You can also visit De Walrus Leeuwarden for a high wine or high beer!
